## Notes for the coordinator failover task (from the review of #42)

- The coordinator never answers JoinGroup with an error yet, so the `preparing` retry path can't happen today. That path sends no heartbeats while the member waits for its next `poll()`. With failover, a cooperative member in the middle of a long batch can then hit `session.timeout.ms` before it retries. Real Kafka does the same, so it is not a bug. The failover tests should cover it, and the liveness precondition may need a session-timeout term.
- Real Kafka answers `COORDINATOR_NOT_AVAILABLE` on JoinGroup by marking the coordinator unknown and looking it up again (FindCoordinator) before retrying. This belongs with the `NOT_COORDINATOR` work.

## Liveness bounds found by the F-3 property

The liveness property (review F-3, lands in PR 2) was run locally against PR 1, 2000 cases. It found no new livelock. It did find two cases where the bound in the review ("batch time under `max.poll.interval.ms`") is too loose for real Kafka. Both traces match the client's behaviour, so they become the property's precondition, not engine changes.

| # | Finding |
|---|---|
| L1 | **Cooperative + auto-commit needs two batches.** At the first `poll()` after it learns of the rebalance, the member sends the onJoinPrepare commit, and that same `poll()` returns the next batch (K12). JoinGroup goes out only at the poll after that batch. Counterexample: 369 records × 59 ms = 21.8 s per batch, rebalance timeout 30.5 s. c1 polled at 69.4 s (sent the commit, got a batch) and would have joined at about 91.2 s. The join phase ended at 79.6 s, so it was removed (`rebalance-timeout`). Eager members return nothing while the commit is in flight, so one batch is enough for them. |
| L2 | **Add one heartbeat interval.** A member learns of a rebalance only from a Heartbeat answer (`REBALANCE_IN_PROGRESS`), so it may start a new batch just before it hears. Counterexample: range, 3.9 s batches, `heartbeat.interval.ms` 2.5 s, rebalance timeout 5.3 s. c3 was removed at 26.37 s and would have joined at 26.42 s. |

The precondition is: `batches × batch time + heartbeat.interval.ms + 1 s < max.poll.interval.ms`, where `batches` is 2 for a cooperative member with auto-commit and 1 otherwise.

| # | Finding |
|---|---|
| L3 | **Settling needs about 4 × the rebalance timeout R, not 2 × R.** A cooperative move takes two rebalances, each up to R. In between, the revoking member needs up to two batches to rejoin, and afterwards each member needs one batch before `poll()` applies the assignment. Counterexample (1500-case run): batches of 10.2 s and R = 26.2 s. Generation 3 completed at 62.7 s, but the run ended at 63.5 s, before c0 and c1 had polled again. Under the precondition, 2 batches < R, so all of this fits in 4 R. | L1 is a good Break-it candidate for a later level: "cooperative isn't free when batches are long."

| # | Decision | Alternatives | Why |

| T1-1 | Broker fields are static configs: read-only in the panel, changeable only in a scene or level. | Editable with "applies at restart". | Kafka reads them only at startup; dynamic broker configs (`kafka-configs --alter`) are out of scope. |
